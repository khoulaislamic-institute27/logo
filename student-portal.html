Lesson 4: Ahkam-e-Noon Saakin & Tanween - Iqlab (الإقلاب)

Definition: Iqlab literally means to convert or transform. In Tajweed, when a Noon Saakin (نْ) or Tanween (ً ٍ ٌ) is followed immediately by the letter Baa (ب), the sound of Noon is converted into a concealed Meem (م) with Ghunnah (nasalization for 2 beats).

كَلَّا ۖ لَيُنبَذَنَّ فِي الْحُطَمَةِ • مِن بَعْدِ مَآ أَرَىٰكَ • عَلِيمٌ بِذَاتِ الصُّدُورِ
Audio Recitation with Precise Tajweed (Qari Alafasy) Listen to the 2-beat concealed Meem sound

Articulation & Pronunciation Tip

When pronouncing Iqlab, gently bring your lips together without pressing them excessively tight, allowing the sound to resonate smoothly through the nasal cavity (Khayshoom).
