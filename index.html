About Our Academy

Nurturing Hearts & Minds with Sacred Quranic Knowledge

At Khoula Islamic Institute, we are dedicated to bringing the divine message of the Quran into homes across the globe. Guided by our motto "Connect with Quran, be near to Rehman", we blend traditional Islamic scholarship with modern virtual classroom technology.

Whether your child is taking their first steps with Noorani Qaida, an adult is seeking fluent Tajweed, or a sister desires private 1-on-1 tutoring, our customized programs provide a safe, respectful, and spiritually uplifting environment.

Authentic Sanad & Tajweed Rigor

Recitation taught according to authentic Hafs 'an Asim transmission with exact throat and tongue Makharij.

Safe & Modest Space for Sisters

Experienced female scholars (Alimahs & Qariahs) dedicated solely to female learners and young girls.

Monthly Progress Tracking & Tarbiyah

Transparent parent report cards covering attendance, memorization retention, and character building.
